I’m a Tibetan nomad with a background in chemistry and materials science. I enjoy working through complicated problems, looking for patterns and following the logic until I can explain how things work.

I recently completed the Erasmus Mundus FAME-AIS master’s program in materials science at TU Darmstadt and Grenoble INP–Phelma.

I carried out my master’s thesis within the [METAL](https://mateis.insa-lyon.fr/fr/page/metal) group at the [MatéIS](https://mateis.insa-lyon.fr/en) laboratory. My research focused on benchmarking MACE foundation models for interstitial solutes in iron and tungsten, exploring their capabilities and limitations in complex atomic environments.

When I'm free, I enjoy skateboarding, hiking, motorcycling and visual design.
