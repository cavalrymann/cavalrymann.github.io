I’m interested in understanding the physical mechanisms behind material behavior and exploring them through simulation.

During my master’s thesis, supervised by [Dr. Petr Grigorev](https://pgrigorev.github.io/), I benchmarked MACE foundation models for interstitial solutes in BCC iron and tungsten. I examined defect behavior, migration barriers, and migration pathways, checking whether the models reproduced the underlying physical mechanisms.

I’d like to build on this experience through further research, exploring how atomistic simulation can help answer new questions about materials.
